What is LuxPDF?

LuxPDF is an open-source, privacy-focused, and completely free suite of online PDF tools. Your documents are processed entirely inside your browser and never uploaded to our servers. There is no registration, no file-data collection, and no usage limit.

Why use our Grayscale or Black & White PDF tool?

  • Two Purpose-Built Modes: Choose smooth grayscale for photographs and general documents, or pure black and white for crisp scans and printing.
  • High-Quality Rendering: Select 150, 200, or print-quality 300 DPI while preserving every page's original physical dimensions.
  • Adaptive Scan Processing: Our black-and-white mode can compensate for shadows, page curl, and uneven scanner lighting on each part of the page.
  • Complete Visual Conversion: Text, vectors, images, transparency, and other visible page content are converted consistently.
  • Private Batch Processing: Convert multiple PDFs locally on your device without uploads or accounts.
  • Printer Friendly: Remove color from handouts, forms, reports, and scanned documents before printing or archiving.

How to use our Grayscale or Black & White PDF tool:

  1. Step 1: Drag and drop one or more PDF files, or click the upload area to select them.
  2. Step 2: Choose Grayscale for smooth shades of gray or Black & White for pure black and pure white output.
  3. Step 3: Select a render quality. High (200 DPI) is recommended for most documents; Print (300 DPI) preserves the finest detail.
  4. Step 4: If using black and white, keep Adaptive processing for scans with uneven backgrounds or choose a fixed threshold for consistent source pages.
  5. Step 5: Click Convert PDF, then download each finished document from the results section.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Are my PDF files uploaded or stored?

No. Rendering, color conversion, and PDF creation all happen locally in your browser. LuxPDF never receives or stores your documents.

What is the difference between grayscale and black and white?

Grayscale retains many shades from black through gray to white, making it best for photographs, gradients, and general documents. Black-and-white mode uses only pure black and pure white pixels, which is ideal for text-heavy scans and monochrome printing.

Which quality setting should I choose?

High (200 DPI) offers an excellent balance for most PDFs. Use Standard (150 DPI) for a smaller output or Print (300 DPI) for fine text, diagrams, and high-quality printing.

Will text and links remain selectable?

No. To ensure every visible color is converted accurately, this tool renders each page as a high-resolution image. The layout and page size are preserved, but text, links, forms, layers, and annotations become part of the page image.

Why does adaptive black and white look better on scans?

Adaptive processing evaluates each small area independently. It can keep text crisp even when a scanned page has shadows, yellowed paper, or uneven illumination that would confuse one fixed threshold.

Does the tool reduce PDF file size?

Sometimes, but it is designed for visual quality and color conversion rather than compression. Resolution, page count, image quality, and the original PDF content all affect the final size. Use Compress PDF afterward if a smaller file is your priority.
